01 / INTRODUCTION

A little about me

I'm currently pursuing graduate studies at the National University of Singapore (NUS), with a growing interest in communication research at the intersection of individuals, organizations, and society.

My academic curiosity centers on how people make sense of complex health information, how organizations communicate in moments of crisis, and how strategic communication can foster understanding and trust.

I enjoy connecting communication theory with real-world questions, and I'm especially interested in research that can make a meaningful difference beyond academia.
