About

A little introduction

My academic interests sit within the broad field of media and communication. I am especially drawn to questions about how organisations communicate, how reputations are formed and challenged, and how media environments influence public understanding.

I enjoy connecting conceptual questions with practical cases, while strengthening my research skills across qualitative and quantitative approaches. I am continuing to explore the research questions and academic pathways I would like to pursue in the next stage of my studies.
